# Content workflow

Turn a creator's real experience or judgment into one publishable piece while preserving voice and creating a measurable learning loop.

## Write in this order

1. Separate confirmed facts, the creator's judgment, and model inference.
2. Build an original-voice draft. Keep concrete scenes, numbers, actions, uncertainty, and self-corrections. Do not invent psychology or outcomes.
3. Confirm the one core claim. Remove material that does not advance it.
4. Improve the opening with a real conflict, result, question, or judgment. Do not replace the creator's position with a generic trend hook.
5. Improve causal transitions. Do not force a three-point lecture, motivational ending, or redundant CTA.
6. Only after the voice is stable, score or predict the final draft.

## Content loop

Use an installed `cheat-on-content` dependency when available:

```text
seed or supplied idea -> draft -> score -> immutable blind prediction
-> shoot -> publish registration -> T+3 retrospective -> rubric update
```

Account-specific profiles, rubrics, drafts, predictions, performance data, and comments belong in the user's workspace, never in this repository.

The approved shooting text may guide terminology and retake detection, but the final working audio remains the caption clock. A reference script must not overwrite what the creator actually said.
